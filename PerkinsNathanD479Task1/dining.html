Explore restaurants serving local and international meals, plus options for buying groceries.

Restaurants on Taniti

Taniti has 10 restaurants with the following cuisine options:

5 Local Restaurants

These primarily serve local fish and rice dishes.

3 American-Style Restaurants

These serve American-style meals.

2 Pan-Asian Restaurants

These serve Pan-Asian cuisine.

Groceries and Convenience Stores

There are two supermarkets, two smaller grocery stores, and one convenience store open 24 hours a day.

Dining on Holidays

Many restaurants and tourist attractions close during Taniti's national holidays. Check local schedules when planning your visit.
