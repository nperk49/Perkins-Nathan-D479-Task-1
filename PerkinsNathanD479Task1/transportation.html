Getting to Taniti

Most visitors arrive by air. The small island airport currently handles small jets and propeller planes, with an expansion planned to accommodate larger jets in future years.

A small cruise ship also docks in Yellow Leaf Bay for one night each week.

Getting Around the Island

Available transportation options
Public busesTaniti City: 5:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. every day.
Private busesServe the rest of the island.
TaxisAvailable in Taniti City.
Rental carsAvailable from a local agency near the airport.
Bicycle rentalsBikes and helmets are available from several vendors. Helmets are required by law.
WalkingTaniti City is fairly flat and walkable, as is the Merriton Landing area.
