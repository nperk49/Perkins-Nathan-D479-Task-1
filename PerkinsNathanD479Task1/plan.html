About the Island

Taniti is a small tropical Pacific island covering fewer than 500 square miles. It has sandy and rocky beaches, a safe harbor, rainforest, mountains, and an active volcano.

The island has an indigenous population of about 20,000. Fishing and agriculture were historically major industries, and tourism has recently become more important.

Visitors often spend time in Taniti City and around Yellow Leaf Bay. Merriton Landing is a developing tourism area north of the bay.
