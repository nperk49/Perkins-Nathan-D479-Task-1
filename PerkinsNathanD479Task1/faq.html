Visitor FAQ

Quick answers about currency, rules, safety, healthcare, and holidays.

Money and Electricity

What currency does Taniti use?

The U.S. dollar is the island's currency. Many businesses also accept euros and yen. Banks offer currency exchange, and many businesses accept major credit cards.

Are electrical outlets compatible with U.S. devices?

Taniti's electrical outlets use 120 volts, the same as in the United States.

Local Rules and Communication

What is the legal drinking age?

The drinking age is 18. The island information says the drinking age is not strictly enforced.

When can alcohol be served or sold?

Alcohol cannot be sold or served from midnight to 9:00 a.m.

Is English widely spoken?

Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English. Much less English is spoken by older residents, especially in rural areas.

Safety and Healthcare

Is Taniti safe for tourists?

Violent crime is very rare. Petty crime, including pickpocketing, has increased as tourism has grown.

What healthcare facilities are available?

Taniti has one hospital and several clinics. Many staff at the hospital speak multiple languages.

Holidays and Closures

Will attractions and restaurants close on holidays?

Taniti observes many national holidays. Restaurants and tourist attractions may be closed, so plan accordingly.

Where can I find public bus hours?

Buses in Taniti City operate every day from 5:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. More information is on the Transportation page.
