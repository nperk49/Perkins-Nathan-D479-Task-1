Things to Do

Enjoy the beaches, rainforest, active volcano, and cultural experiences of Taniti.

Natural Attractions

Beaches

Visit the white, sandy beaches around Yellow Leaf Bay or explore the island's sandy and rocky coastline.

Rainforest

Explore lush tropical rainforest on hiking trails or enjoy zip-lining opportunities.

Volcano

Taniti's mountainous interior includes a small, active volcano that tourists can visit.

Entertainment and Sightseeing

There is more to Taniti than beaches and rainforest adventures.

  • Chartered fishing, snorkeling, boat tours, and helicopter rides
  • Local history museum and art galleries
  • Rainforest zip-lining, island bus tours, and sightseeing
  • Pubs, a microbrewery, a dance club, a movie theater, an arcade, and bowling

Many activities are located around Merriton Landing, north of Yellow Leaf Bay. A nine-hole golf course is planned for the future.
